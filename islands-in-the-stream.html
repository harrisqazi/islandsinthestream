🌺 Our Story

Richmond's Newest Caribbean Destination

Nestled in the heart of downtown Richmond at 144 E. Main Street, Islands in the Stream brings the vibrant flavors and soulful spirit of the Caribbean straight to the Bluegrass State.

From our beloved jerk chicken and coconut shrimp to fresh conch fritters and rich oxtail soup — every dish is a journey. We've created a space where creative Caribbean cuisine meets Kentucky warmth, with stunning murals, a full bar, and a patio that makes every evening feel like an island getaway.
