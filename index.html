🌺 Our Story

Richmond's Newest Caribbean Destination

Nestled in the heart of downtown Richmond at 144 E. Main Street, Islands in the Stream brings the vibrant flavors and soulful spirit of the Caribbean straight to the Bluegrass State.

From jerk chicken and coconut shrimp to conch fritters and island-inspired entrées — every dish is a journey. We've created a space where Caribbean cuisine meets Kentucky warmth, with a full bar and a welcoming downtown atmosphere.
